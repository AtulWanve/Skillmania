# Wait What

The user did not understand or follow your last message. Stop immediately and re-pitch it.

## Rules

1. **Drop the Jargon**:
   - Strip out academic, architectural, and complex terminology.
   - Use plain, direct English (Simplified Technical English).

2. **Give the High-Level Context**:
   - Explain what problem we are actually trying to solve in one sentence.
   - Ground the explanation in the project's real domain and concrete files.

3. **Format as What / Why / Next**:
   - **What happened / What this is**: 1-2 plain sentences.
   - **Why it matters**: What breaks or what improves.
   - **The choice or next action**: Simple, concrete options for what to do next.
